Verified Clinical Case Ledger

Eight documented child & adolescent trajectories (FY 2024–25)

Extracted directly from SAMADHAN’s 2023–24 and 2024–25 clinical review dossiers. Each case reflects structured IEP goals across motor, sensory, speech, and classroom readiness domains.
Vivaan Awasthi (6 years)SMAS Auto Leasing ABA Therapy Room & Speech Therapy
Diagnosis / Profile: Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)Baseline: Entered with limited verbal expression, difficulty maintaining visual tracking, and high sensory restlessness in structured classroom settings.Intervention: Daily individualized sessions inside the new climate-controlled SMAS ABA Therapy Room using oral-motor tools, thematic flashcards, visual learning boards, and foam tunnel climbers.
Measured Outcome (FY 2024–25): Achieved measurable gains in vocalization, visual tracking, gross motor balance, and independent engagement with daily living (ADL) wall panels.
Rihansh (4 years)Early Intervention & SMAS ABA Sensory Suite
Diagnosis / Profile: Global Developmental Delay (GDD)Baseline: Experienced acute separation anxiety when parting from his mother and avoided unfamiliar tactile textures and group activities.Intervention: Graduated sensory desensitization using the 12-texture mats from Sugamya Quissa Pitara, mini-trampoline proprioceptive play, foam climbers, and abacus counting frames.
Measured Outcome (FY 2024–25): Overcame separation anxiety within months; now enters the therapy room independently and initiates play with peers and educators.
Ishan Rajoura (9 years)Early Intervention, Physiotherapy, Speech & Special Education
Diagnosis / Profile: Apraxia Cerebral Palsy & HypotoniaBaseline: Contracted acute jaundice at age 2 in Dakshinpuri, losing motor control, posture, and speech clarity.Intervention: Identified during a door-to-door survey. Completed 3 years of intensive physiotherapy, speech therapy, and 1:5 Individual Education Plan (IEP) schooling at SAMADHAN.
Measured Outcome (Verified Case Study): Successfully admitted into a mainstream classroom at Balwant Rai Mehta School in Dakshinpuri.
Roopali (9 years)Multidisciplinary Rehabilitation, OT, Physiotherapy & Nutrition
Diagnosis / Profile: Cerebral Palsy (Spastic Quadriplegia)Baseline: Joined at age 6 lying flat on her back, unable to hold her head up, sit, grasp objects, or chew solid food.Intervention: Three years of sustained occupational therapy, postural positioning, oral-motor feeding therapy, and hands-on caregiver training for her mother Sangeeta.
Measured Outcome (Verified Case Study): Now sits with minimal support, grasps toys, eats solid meals independently, and participates in group sessions.
Sneha (6 years)Dakshinpuri Early Intervention & Pre-School Readiness Unit
Diagnosis / Profile: Down SyndromeBaseline: Joined the Dakshinpuri Early Intervention Unit with delayed speech, hypotonia, and social withdrawal.Intervention: Enrolled in daily group sensory play, music and rhythm therapy, and pre-school readiness exercises.
Measured Outcome (FY 2023–24): Became the expressive leader of her classroom cohort, participating in cultural dances, group songs, and independent self-care.
Shubhan (5 years)Home-Based & Centre-Based Early Intervention + Family Counseling
Diagnosis / Profile: Global Developmental Delay (GDD)Baseline: Could neither stand nor speak at intake; his young mother Tabassum faced severe household blame in Dakshinpuri.Intervention: Outreach worker Sarojini counseled the entire family in Dakshinpuri and enrolled Shubhan in daily gait training, speech therapy, and group rhythm sessions.
Measured Outcome (Verified Case Study): Now walks independently, sings rhymes, and speaks in full sentences, eliminating maternal stigma at home.
Payal & Mother Usha (Identified at age 2)3-Year Home-Based Early Intervention + Mother's SHG Employment
Diagnosis / Profile: Intergenerational IDDBaseline: Identified at age 2 in Madangir after her father passed away; her mother Usha also had an intellectual disability and zero income.Intervention: Home-based educator Neelam trained Payal at home for 3 years before moving her into center-based schooling, while SAMADHAN employed mother Usha in its Women's Self-Help Group.
Measured Outcome (Verified Case Study): Kept mother and daughter together with a dignified livelihood in the Women's SHG and continuous special education.
Yuvraj, Yagya, Aarish, Bhavya & Pratham (4 to 11 years)DataFlow Group Child Sponsorship Cohort
Diagnosis / Profile: CP, Autism & GDD CohortBaseline: Five children from low-income families requiring multi-domain occupational, speech, and behavioural therapy.Intervention: Full-year sponsored clinical therapy, IEP tracking, and parent counseling documented in the FY 2023–24 Annual Report.
Measured Outcome (FY 2023–24): All 5 children recorded verified improvements in fine motor coordination, speech clarity, listening responsiveness, and peer socialization.

Society for the Aid of Mentally and Developmentally Handicapped (SAMADHAN). Founded in December 1981 by clinical psychologist Pramila Balasundaram in Dakshinpuri resettlement colony, New Delhi. Providing early intervention, ABA therapy, special education, maternal mental health counseling, and vocational pathways for children with intellectual and developmental disabilities.
