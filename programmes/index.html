7 Integrated Programmes · FY 2024–25 Clinical Protocols

Multidisciplinary therapy, inclusive education, and maternal mental health under one roof.

SAMADHAN’s clinical pipeline follows the child and mother from neonatal developmental screening (0–6 years) through special education and mainstream school transition (6–18 years) into adult sheltered vocational production (18+ years).
01 · Early Intervention (0–6 yrs)Cohort: 0 to 6 yearsLocation: Dakshinpuri & Dwarka Centres

Early Intervention & Infant Stimulation (Disha Scheme)

Identifies developmental delays during the peak neuroplasticity window. For infants under 3, trained community workers visit homes to counsel mothers on acceptance, breastfeeding, Oral Rehydration (ORD), infant nutrition, and daily motor exercises. Children aged 3–6 transition into our pediatric clinical pre-school.
Clinical Modalities & Protocols
[1]Enrolled under The National Trust's DISHA Early Intervention and School Readiness Scheme (0–10 yrs).
[2]Maternal counseling to eliminate family stigma alongside high-protein Nutri-Mix supplementation.
[3]Pediatrician-supervised developmental screening, sensory stimulation, and inclusive playschool.
AUDITED FY 2024–25 MILESTONE · The National Trust (Disha) & Individual 80G SponsorsToddlers like Shubhan and Rihansh progressed from acute motor/sensory delays to independent walking and peer play.

02 · ABA, OT, Speech & Physio ClinicCohort: 0 to 16 yearsLocation: Dwarka Sector 2 & Dakshinpuri

Multidisciplinary Rehabilitation & SMAS ABA Therapy Clinic

Brings together a Pediatrician, Occupational Therapist, Speech & Hearing Therapist, Physiotherapist, Clinical Psychologist, and Special Educators under one roof for families who cannot afford ₹1,500/session private hospital rates.
Clinical Modalities & Protocols
[1]Climate-controlled SMAS Auto Leasing Applied Behaviour Analysis (ABA) Room with standing frames, foam tunnel climbers, scooter boards, and oral-motor kits.
[2]Custom orthotic and prosthetic assistive devices (CP chairs, walkers, parallel bars, gait training).
[3]Niramaya Health Insurance enrollment providing up to ₹1,00,000/year medical coverage per child.

03 · 1:5 Special Education & Quissa PitaraCohort: 6 to 14 yearsLocation: Dakshinpuri & Dwarka Classrooms

Special & Inclusive Education (Sugamya Quissa Pitara)

A structured 5-day-a-week school environment where every child receives a tailored Individual Education Plan (IEP). Educators craft low-cost teaching aids from local materials and integrate the JCB Literature Foundation Sugamya Quissa Pitara multisensory trunk.
Clinical Modalities & Protocols
[1]1:5 teacher-to-student ratio—contrasting with 1:45 overcrowded local classrooms.
[2]65 tactile games, 12-texture sensory mats, braille/large-font books, and daily living (ADL) wall panels.
[3]Active mainstream school transition pathway into local schools (Balwant Rai Mehta School, Sivas Public School).
AUDITED FY 2024–25 MILESTONE · The JCB Literature Foundation & Access For AllSelected as India's first launch site for JCB Literature Foundation's Sugamya Quissa Pitara inclusive learning box.

04 · Pre-Vocational & ICT LabCohort: 12 to 18+ yearsLocation: Dakshinpuri & Dwarka Vocational Units

Pre-Vocational Skills & Computer Literacy Unit

Prepares adolescents with intellectual and developmental disabilities for dignified economic participation, functional independence, and assisted co-production alongside our women's collectives.
Clinical Modalities & Protocols

[2]Functional computer lab literacy, urban kitchen gardening, hydroponics, and housekeeping routines.
[3]Task-analyzed co-production in the Papier-Mâché unit where youth with severe IDD lead paper soaking, pulping, and base moulding.

05 · THE A.R.T.S. DramatherapyCohort: 4 to 18 yearsLocation: Dakshinpuri, Dwarka & IIC New Delhi

THE A.R.T.S. Dramatherapy & Expressive Movement

Pioneered in India in 1992 by Founder Pramila Balasundaram in collaboration with Dr. Sue Jennings and the British Institute of Drama Therapists. Translates Western dramatherapy into the Indian cultural idiom through story dramatization, rhythm, puppet theatre, and mural art.
Clinical Modalities & Protocols
[1]THE A.R.T.S. (Art, Recreation, Therapy, and Song) builds emotional regulation, self-esteem, and anti-bullying confidence.
[2]2024–25 partnership with Art for Change Foundation (Director Isaac Gregan) creating the Dwarka Centre hallway mural.
[3]Landmark public performances including 'The Carnival of Animals', 'The Little Butterfly', 'Awaken the Magic', and 'A Walk to Remember' at India International Centre (IIC).

07 · Community Outreach & SurveysCohort: 0 to 18 years + Community FamiliesLocation: South & South-West Delhi Resettlement Colonies

Community-Based Rehabilitation (CBR) & Door-to-Door Surveys

Rather than waiting for families in urban resettlement colonies to find a clinic, SAMADHAN trains local women from the community as outreach survey workers to go door-to-door, identify hidden disabilities early, and sensitize MCD schools and dispensaries.
Clinical Modalities & Protocols
[1]Surveyed 5,751+ households, 153 primary schools (MCD, government, private), and 39 clinics/dispensaries in recorded baseline sweeps.
[2]Training of Trainers (ToT) converting experienced mothers and ASHA workers into paid community faculty.
[3]Street puppet theatre, free pediatric health camps, epilepsy awareness, and Legal Literacy Camps with Delhi Legal Services Authority.
