Zero-Barrier Clinical Referral Workflow

What happens when a parent brings a child to SAMADHAN?

Many parents arrive anxious after referrals from Safdarjung Hospital, AIIMS, Kalawati Saran, or local Anganwadi workers. Our intake process is designed to give clarity within 48 hours.
STEP 01Walk-In & Developmental ScreeningA clinical social worker and special educator record birth history, developmental milestones, and sensory or motor concerns.
STEP 02Psychological & Therapy EvaluationStandardized assessment by our clinical psychologist, occupational therapist, and speech educator to establish baseline functioning.
STEP 03Individualized Education Plan (IEP)A customized 90-day clinical and home-training plan is co-created with the child's mother, plus UDID / Niramaya insurance assistance.
STEP 04Daily Therapy & Maternal SupportThe child enters the Early Intervention (0–6) or Disha Special Education (6–18) cohort while the mother joins peer mental health counseling.
