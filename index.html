Early developmental screening, speech therapy, ABA, and special education in South & West Delhi.

If your child (0 to 18 years) shows delays in sitting, walking, speech, eye contact, sensory processing, or school learning, our clinical psychologists and occupational therapists provide structured assessments. Cradle-to-Livelihood Intervention Model

Seven integrated clinical and community programmes.

Instead of treating intellectual disability in isolation, SAMADHAN combines infant neurological stimulation, school readiness, maternal psychiatry, and sheltered adult enterprise under one clinical roof.
Full Clinical Specifications →
Ages 0 to 6 years45+ Infants & Toddlers

Early Intervention & Infant Stimulation (Disha Scheme)

Identifies developmental delays during the peak neuroplasticity window. For infants under 3, trained community workers visit homes to counsel mothers on acceptance, breastfeeding, Oral Rehydration (ORD), infant nutrition, and daily motor exercises. Children aged 3–6 transition into our pediatric clinical pre-school.
FY 2024–25: Toddlers like Shubhan and Rihansh progressed from acute motor/sensory delays to independent walking and peer play.

Longitudinal IEP Case Outcomes · FY 2024–25

Measured clinical progress, child by child.

Every child enrolled at SAMADHAN receives a quarterly Individualized Education Plan (IEP) review. Below are four documented trajectories from our 2024–2025 clinical audit.
Read All 8 Case Audits →
V
Vivaan Awasthi · 6 yearsSMAS Auto Leasing ABA Therapy Room & Speech Therapy
Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
Baseline at Intake: Entered with limited verbal expression, difficulty maintaining visual tracking, and high sensory restlessness in structured classroom settings.Clinical Intervention: Daily individualized sessions inside the new climate-controlled SMAS ABA Therapy Room using oral-motor tools, thematic flashcards, visual learning boards, and foam tunnel climbers.
Documented Outcome (FY 2024–25): Achieved measurable gains in vocalization, visual tracking, gross motor balance, and independent engagement with daily living (ADL) wall panels.
R
Rihansh · 4 yearsEarly Intervention & SMAS ABA Sensory Suite
Global Developmental Delay (GDD)
Baseline at Intake: Experienced acute separation anxiety when parting from his mother and avoided unfamiliar tactile textures and group activities.Clinical Intervention: Graduated sensory desensitization using the 12-texture mats from Sugamya Quissa Pitara, mini-trampoline proprioceptive play, foam climbers, and abacus counting frames.
Documented Outcome (FY 2024–25): Overcame separation anxiety within months; now enters the therapy room independently and initiates play with peers and educators.
I
Ishan Rajoura · 9 yearsEarly Intervention, Physiotherapy, Speech & Special Education
Apraxia Cerebral Palsy & Hypotonia
Baseline at Intake: Contracted acute jaundice at age 2 in Dakshinpuri, losing motor control, posture, and speech clarity.Clinical Intervention: Identified during a door-to-door survey. Completed 3 years of intensive physiotherapy, speech therapy, and 1:5 Individual Education Plan (IEP) schooling at SAMADHAN.
Documented Outcome (Verified Case Study): Successfully admitted into a mainstream classroom at Balwant Rai Mehta School in Dakshinpuri.
R
Roopali · 9 yearsMultidisciplinary Rehabilitation, OT, Physiotherapy & Nutrition
Cerebral Palsy (Spastic Quadriplegia)
Baseline at Intake: Joined at age 6 lying flat on her back, unable to hold her head up, sit, grasp objects, or chew solid food.Clinical Intervention: Three years of sustained occupational therapy, postural positioning, oral-motor feeding therapy, and hands-on caregiver training for her mother Sangeeta.
Documented Outcome (Verified Case Study): Now sits with minimal support, grasps toys, eats solid meals independently, and participates in group sessions.
