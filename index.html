A child's path

From a knock on the door to a classroom

  1. 1Find

    We knock on doors

    Community workers visit homes and local schools to find children who need help.Community outreach
  2. 2Assess

    Doctors and therapists meet the child

    A paediatrician, therapists and a psychologist see how the child moves, speaks and learns.Therapy
  3. 3Early years

    Help in the first years

    Parents learn exercises to do at home every day, with regular visits to the centre.Early intervention
  4. 4School

    Small classes

    About one teacher to five children. When a child is ready, we help them join a regular school.Special education
  5. 5Teenagers

    Practical skills, ages 12 to 16

    Block printing, card making, bookbinding and gardening.Pre-vocational unit
