# What we need from SAMADHAN

The preview site is built from the 2020-21 annual report and the current website. These items replace placeholders or unlock features. Items 1 to 5 block launch.

## Blocks launch

1. **Numbers for 2024-25.** Children in early intervention, special education, pre-vocational training, therapy clinics; mothers in the women's group; children who joined mainstream school. The site currently shows the 2020-21 figures, labelled as 2020-21.
2. **Registration numbers.** 80G (number and validity), 12A, CSR-1, NGO Darpan ID, PAN. These go in the trust bar and the accountability page. Corporate donors look for them first.
3. **Consent for photos and stories.** For each photo marked "confirm" in `content/assets.json`, a yes or no from the family or adult shown. For the nine success stories on the old site, a yes or no to republishing with first name only.
4. **Donation link.** The Razorpay (or other) payment link behind the "Donate From India" button, and whether UPI is accepted. We will not build a checkout without it.
5. **Spelling and dates.** Founder's name (Prameela or Pramila), World Bank award year (2001 or 2002), which email inbox is checked daily.

## Makes the site better

6. **Unit costs for giving tiers.** What one month of therapy for one child costs; one term of special education; one assistive device; one month of a mother's training. Rough is fine. We show "₹X pays for Y" only with your numbers.
7. **Original photos.** Full-size files from your phones or cameras, especially: the spice and laddoo units at work, the garden, therapy sessions, the two buildings. Our photos come from PDFs and are small.
8. **Logo file.** The original logo (AI, EPS, SVG or a large PNG). We rebuilt it from the annual report.
9. **Hindi review.** Someone fluent to read the Hindi "For families" page.
10. **Social links.** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube handles you want shown.
11. **Clinic timings.** Days and hours for both centres, and the phone or WhatsApp number parents should use.
12. **Product prices.** Spices, laddoos, papier-mache: what you sell, prices, how people order.

## For launch day

13. **Domain access.** Who controls samadhanindia.org DNS (Hostinger? Cloudflare?). We need to point it to the new site.
14. **Google Search Console and Analytics.** Add ryan@amalgam-inc.com as a user so we can move the site without losing search ranking.
15. **WordPress export.** If you can, a full media export from WordPress (Tools, Export). Cloudflare blocks us from downloading the images on the current site.
