## History (WEB /milestones/ page source, AR21)

1981 registered · 1983 first special education unit, Dakshinpuri · 1986 first early intervention workshop · 1988 first Indian member of Inclusion International · 1989 vocational unit · 1990 rehabilitation clinic · 1992 first national drama therapy workshop · 1994 Dakshinpuri centre opened by Dr Kiran Bedi (foundation stone 1993, with Lebenshilfe) · 1998 Inclusion International award; community-based rehabilitation begins · 2000 income generation for mothers · 2001 World Bank award · 2003 Dwarka building completed · 2005 Dubai Best Practice award · 2011 SAMADHAN elected President of AFID · 2013 hosted 21st AFID conference · 2018 Coffee Because award · 2020 lockdown: therapy moved to WhatsApp and video calls; pre-vocational units opened; garden project starts · 2026 45 years.

## Language rules

- Person-first: "children with intellectual disabilities", never "the mentally handicapped", "the disabled", "suffering from".
- "Down syndrome" (not "Downs Syndrome").
- Children's surnames, diagnoses beyond what the family published, and medical histories are not shown. First names only, and only where the annual report already published them.
