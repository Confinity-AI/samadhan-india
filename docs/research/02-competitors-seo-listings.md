# SAMADHAN India: competitive, SEO and partnership research (Oct 2026)

## Executive takeaways

1. **For queries that name the brand, Samadhan is effectively invisible.** A search for "samadhan ngo" brought up three other organisations and no Samadhan India page:
   - the Dehradun legal-aid NGO ([samadhanngo.org](http://www.samadhanngo.org/));
   - Samadhan Abhiyan, a child-protection (POCSO) awareness NGO in Delhi ([samadhanabhiyan.org](https://samadhanabhiyan.org/));
   - Samadhan Foundation, a de-addiction NGO in Delhi that is already on Give.do ([give.do/ngos/samadhan-foundation](https://give.do/ngos/samadhan-foundation)).

   Fixing this needs a consistent brand ("SAMADHAN India — intellectual disability, Delhi"), structured data, Google Business Profiles and claimed directory listings.

2. **The topic queries are winnable.** For "intellectual disability NGO Delhi", samadhanindia.org came back as result 1, ahead of Muskaan, Manovikas and Tamana. Tamana dominates autism, special-school and early-intervention queries. Samadhan has the content advantage in its niche (intellectual disability plus poverty plus mothers) but publishes almost no depth around it.

3. **Manovikas is the most dangerous competitor.** Its positioning is almost the same as Samadhan's: intellectual and developmental disabilities, early intervention, free for families below the poverty line. Its new site is the best in the Delhi sector, with plain-language "Easy Read" text, a live impact dashboard and every registration number displayed.

4. **There's money on the table from schemes Samadhan already holds.** The National Trust released only ₹2.39L, ₹1.76L, ₹3.60L and ₹4.05L to Samadhan under Disha for FY22-23 through FY25-26. Comparable Disha holders got about ₹12–13L a year ([National Trust fund-release PDF](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3300891a62162b960cf02ce3827bb363c/uploads/2026/06/202606031229041822.pdf)).

5. **Domestic donation UX is the biggest conversion gap.** `samadhanindia.org/donate/` returns a 404. The only giving page I found on the new site is international bank details ([/international-donations/](https://samadhanindia.org/international-donations/)), with no one-click Indian rupee donation, no monthly giving and no instant 80G receipt.

## 5. Design inspiration: patterns worth borrowing

1. **Price tiers in units of outcome.** Akshaya Patra: "₹1,500 to feed 1 child" for a year, laddered up to ₹90,000 ([page](https://www.akshayapatra.org/donate-and-save-tax)). CBM: ₹900 a month means "1 child for 12 months" ([page](https://www.cbmindia.org/make-a-donation2/)). Muskurahat sells months of education, from ₹1,500 for one month to ₹18,000 for twelve ([page](https://www.muskurahat.org.in/donate?r=adit7887)). Samadhan needs its own validated costs, such as a month of speech therapy or a mother's training cohort. Delhi benchmarks: DCCW ₹2,000 a month, Amar Jyoti ₹20,000 a year, DSWSC ₹70,000 a year.
2. **Monthly giving as a named community.** charity: water's "The Spring": "$20 a month… an entire family" with member testimonials ([page](https://www.charitywater.org/the-spring)). A "Friends of SAMADHAN" monthly circle would extend the existing Australian group.
3. **Separate the operations story from the impact story.** charity: water's 100% Model funds operations from a private donor circle, "The Well" ([page](https://www.charitywater.org/our-approach/100-percent-model)). A lighter version for Samadhan: a published "where ₹100 goes" breakdown alongside the audited financials.
4. **A live impact dashboard rather than static counters.** Manovikas: "Live summaries from our management system… 160 people at our events in 2026, as on 9 Oct" ([home](https://site.manovikasfamily.org/)).
5. **Lifetime counters with defined audiences.** Ummeed: 14,500+ children, 3,400+ caregivers, 8,600+ professionals, 435,000 reached indirectly ([home](https://ummeed.org/)). Samadhan's natural trio is children, mothers trained, and community workers.
6. **Easy Read dual-layer copy.** Manovikas puts a plain-language line under every paragraph ([about](https://site.manovikasfamily.org/about)). For an intellectual-disability organisation this is accessibility and dignity, not decoration.
7. **A parent admissions funnel next to the donor funnel.** Manovikas A3C: "enquire → free assessment → family plan", with WhatsApp and named contacts ([A3C](https://site.manovikasfamily.org/a3c)). Tamana runs an admissions banner sitewide ([home](https://tamana.ngo/)).
8. **A trust bar with registration numbers.** Manovikas lists 80G, 12A, FCRA, NGO Darpan, CSR and National Trust IDs in-line ([about](https://site.manovikasfamily.org/about)).
9. **Chaptered, story-led scroll.** Awwwards Site of the Day "In My World" uses chapters with dot navigation ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/in-my-world)). The/Nudge pairs named first-person quotes with a place ("Sarvar Nisha, Barabanki, UP") ([home](https://www.thenudge.org/)). That suits mothers' stories from Dakshinpuri and Dwarka.
