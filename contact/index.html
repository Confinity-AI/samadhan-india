Society for the Aid of Mentally and Developmentally Handicapped (SAMADHAN). Founded in December 1981 by clinical psychologist Pramila Balasundaram in Dakshinpuri resettlement colony, New Delhi. Providing early intervention, ABA therapy, special education, maternal mental health counseling, and vocational pathways for children with intellectual and developmental disabilities.
